Evaluation Guide

Operational guide and scoring reference for academic evaluators.

Human-in-the-Loop Philosophy

Mulyankan is designed as an advisory evaluation system. The automated score suggestions, citation matches, and plagiarism detections are generated to reduce manual grading fatigue and improve grading consistency.

Important: AI-generated scores and metrics are baseline recommendations. As an evaluator, your academic judgment is the final authority. You have complete autonomy to override scores and modify remarks prior to submission.
Question Scoring Mechanics

Answers are analyzed on a per-question basis across three core dimensional metrics:

Content Coverage

Measures conceptual correctness, technical depth, inclusion of key terminology, and alignment with course learning objectives.

Linguistic Quality

Evaluates grammatical accuracy, professional vocabulary, syntax, and sentence flow appropriate for higher education.

Presentation & Structure

Assesses logical organization, point-by-point clarity, use of structured headings/diagrams, and overall legibility.

Plagiarism & Integrity Logic

The integrity engine performs three distinct algorithmic checks on every submission:

  • Textbook Similarit: Measures direct phrase matches against prescribed university course blocks and units using ngram overlap. High matches suggest verbatim copying from official study material. Only matches above 70% are flagged to avoid penalizing standard textbook definitons.
  • Peer Similarity: Compares student responses across the current academic batch using paired ngram overlap. A directional overalp formula is used so that penalty accurately reflects the extent of plagiarism. Matched peer enrollment IDs are displayed for cross-verification.
  • AI Detection: Analyzes sentence perplexity and variation to estimate the probability the text was generated by a language model. This is done using coai-roberta-ai-detector model from HuggingFace that was trained specifically on academic textual data.

The system treats textbook duplication and peer collusion with equal severity by selecting the dominant overlap metric:

Plagiarism Score = max(Textbook Overlap, Peer Similarity)
Submissions exceeding the 70% threshold trigger a linear penalty deduction proportional to the excess overlap. Overlap up to 70% is tolerated as standard conceptual phrasing, while anything abouve 70% threshold incurs deduction proportionally such that 100% plagiarized work results in a complete deduction or 0 marks.

Penalty Policy Note: Automatic marks deductions are applied strictly for verified textbook and peer-to-peer matches as there are deterministic. AI language detection percentages are provided strictly as an advisory flag and do not trigger automatic point deductions. Exercise caution and human judgement while applying deductions as AI detectors can produce false-positives.
Step-by-Step Evaluation Protocol
  1. Inspect Answer PDF: Scroll through the left PDF pane to verify textual clarity, completeness, and original student diagrams.
  2. Review Question-wise Evaluation: Expand each question breakdown to inspect sub-scores, specific AI feedback, textbook citations, and flagged peer matches.
  3. Adjust Human Final Score: Enter your final awarded score in the review input box. You may accept the suggested total or override it based on your assessment.
  4. Provide Evaluator Remarks: You can copy and edit the AI-generated rationale or draft original feedback for official record keeping.
  5. Submit Review: Click Submit & Finalize Review to lock the score and finalize the evaluation.
